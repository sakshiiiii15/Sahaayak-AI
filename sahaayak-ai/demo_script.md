# Sahaayak AI Demo Recording Script

## Demo setup
- Open the frontend app in your browser.
- Ensure the backend Flask API is running at `http://127.0.0.1:5000`.
- Go to the Analyzer tab.

## Demo actions
1. Click the textarea to focus it.
2. Click **Demo Scam** to auto-fill a realistic scam message.
3. Click **Analyze Everything**.
4. Wait for the result card to appear.
5. Highlight the final outputs:
   - `SCAM` status
   - `RISK CONFIDENCE` score
   - simplified safety advice
   - explanation bullets or risk reasons
6. End with the final result clearly visible for 3-4 seconds.

## Narration script
1. "This is the Sahaayak AI scam detection dashboard."
2. "I am using a realistic overdue bill scam message with a shortened payment link."
3. "Now I click Analyze Everything to run the detection engine."
4. "The system checks scam patterns, urgency, link risk, and scam markers."
5. "It returns a clear `SCAM` verdict with a high confidence score."
6. "The app also gives safety guidance and explains why the message is dangerous."

## Recommended recording flow
- 0–5 sec: show app and click `Demo Scam`
- 5–12 sec: click `Analyze Everything` and wait for processing
- 12–25 sec: show result card and point out the score/status/explanation
- 25–30 sec: final frame with the verdict visible

## Notes
- If you want a smoother capture, use a screen recorder and keep the cursor movements minimal.
- The new `Demo Scam` button auto-fills the exact sample text for the clip.
